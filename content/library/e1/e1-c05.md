## The Crux

An explicit, empirically equivalent hidden-variable completion of nonrelativistic quantum mechanics: particles possess definite positions guided by the wavefunction through $\dot{\mathbf{q}}_k = (\hbar / m_k)\, \mathrm{Im}\,(\nabla_k \psi / \psi)$, with the quantum equilibrium distribution $|\psi|^2$. Its existence flatly contradicted the received reading of von Neumann's impossibility proof — and, decisive for the lineage, the theory is manifestly *nonlocal*: the velocity of particle $k$ depends instantaneously on all other positions. Bohm had inadvertently exhibited the trade-off Bell would prove mandatory.

## Consensus Shift

Before: hidden variables were believed proven impossible. After: they were exhibited, and the question became *which properties* (locality, noncontextuality) any such theory must sacrifice — exactly the question Movement 2 answers. Immediate reception was hostile or silent; the paper's status was rehabilitated by Bell in print.
