## The Crux

The one-molecule engine: a single molecule in a box, a partition, a measurement of which side the molecule occupies, and an extraction of work $k_B T \ln 2$ per cycle — apparently violating the second law unless the acquisition or processing of one binary digit of information carries a compensating entropy cost of $k_B \ln 2$. This is the first equation in physics in which "bit" and "entropy" appear on the same ledger.

- Classical statistical mechanics of a single degree of freedom
- Isothermal work extraction

## Active Replication

**Tier 2.** Simulate the Szilard cycle with an overdamped Langevin particle in a switchable double-well potential; measure the work distribution over many cycles and verify $\langle W \rangle \le k_B T \ln 2$, approaching equality in the quasistatic limit. Acceptance criterion: the work histogram and its mean-versus-driving-rate curve. Effort: one to two days.
