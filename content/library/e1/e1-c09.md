## The Crux

Logical irreversibility implies thermodynamic irreversibility: erasing one bit (a two-to-one map on logical states) requires dissipating at least $k_B T \ln 2$ of heat into the environment; logically reversible operations carry no such floor. The principle localizes Szilard's cost in the *reset*, not the measurement — the correction that later dissolves Maxwell's demon.
