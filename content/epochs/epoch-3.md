**Central mathematical object:** the quantum Turing machine and the quantum circuit; query complexity.

Deutsch (1985) defined universal quantum computation; Deutsch–Jozsa (1992), Bernstein–Vazirani (1993), and Simon (1994) built the oracle separations; Shor (1994) detonated them into the factoring algorithm. The mathematics that had to evolve here was complexity theory over amplitudes: interference as a computational resource, the Fourier transform over abelian groups as the engine. Shor's algorithm is the hinge of the entire field's sociology: after 1994, decoherence was no longer a philosophical nuisance but the obstacle to a technology, which made Epoch IV inevitable.
