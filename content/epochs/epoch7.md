**Central mathematical object:** the quantum trajectory ensemble; the measurement-induced phase transition; the adaptive (feedback) channel.

Preskill's NISQ framing (2018) redefined the near term. Independently, a new statistical mechanics emerged: circuits with interleaved unitaries and measurements exhibit measurement-induced phase transitions in the entanglement structure of individual trajectories (Li–Chen–Fisher; Skinner–Ruhman–Nahum, 2018–19), invisible to the averaged density matrix. Gaussian fermionic versions — free-fermion monitored circuits analyzed through exactly the covariance-matrix contraction machinery run on HTCondor — revealed symmetry-class-dependent phase diagrams, tying Epoch VII directly back to the tenfold way of Epoch VI. The adaptive turn (feedback conditioned on measurement outcomes, i.e., steering toward target states, including topological ones) is the current frontier and this thesis lineage: measurement plus feedforward as a preparation and stabilization technology for topological order. The mathematics that had to evolve: replica statistical mechanics of trajectories, Gaussian tensor networks, and the theory of quantum channels with classical feedback.
